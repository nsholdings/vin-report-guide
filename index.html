Know what you are buying before the money moves

A used car can look clean and still carry a salvage title, a rolled-back odometer, an unreleased lien, or an open safety recall. Most of that history is recorded somewhere. This site explains where to look, which checks are free, and where the free checks stop.

The short version

Run the free checks first. They cost nothing and they catch the worst cases. If the car survives them and you are still serious, a paid history report fills in the parts the free tools do not cover — auction records, service entries, lien records, and title history across states.

Then, and only then, pay a mechanic for a pre-purchase inspection. Paperwork is cheap to check. Inspections are not.

Have a VIN in hand? A full history report pulls title, odometer, accident, theft, salvage, lien and auction records into one place.
