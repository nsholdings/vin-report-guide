About this site

What this is

VIN Report Guide is an independent guide to checking a used vehicle's recorded history before buying it. It covers what the free government and industry tools do, where they stop, and what a paid history report adds.

Who runs it

NS Holdings, an independent operator. Contact: nsholdings0523@gmail.com

How the content is researched

  • Every factual claim about a service comes from that service's own published documentation, linked inline.
  • The date each page was last checked is stated on the page.
  • This site does not publish reviews of products it has not used as if it had used them. Where a paid product is recommended, the recommendation is based on what the provider states it covers, and that is how it is described.
  • Corrections are welcome at the address above and are made promptly.

How it is funded

Through affiliate commissions. See the advertising disclosure for the full explanation. Commissions do not change which tools are described or how their limitations are presented — the free options are covered in as much detail as the paid one, and their limits are stated in the operators' own words.

Not affiliated

No legal advice

Title law, lemon law and disclosure requirements vary by state and change over time. Nothing here is legal advice. For a specific transaction, consult your state's motor vehicle agency or a qualified attorney.
