Buying a used car in Nebraska: Previously Salvaged, and the $12,500 line

Last checked November 1, 2026

Nebraska's wording is worth learning because it is not the wording most buyers expect. The post-repair brand does not say rebuilt. It says Previously Salvaged, and it sits under the Legends designation on the title.

Which cars are in scope

A salvage vehicle is a late-model vehicle whose repair cost exceeds 75% of the retail value at the time it was damaged. And Nebraska's definition of a late-model vehicle has two limbs (Nebraska DMV):

  • Seven years old or less by model year; or
  • A retail value above $12,500 — rising to $13,000 on 1 January 2030.

That second limb is the one to remember. An expensive car stays inside the scheme long after it has passed the seven-year mark. A cheap car drops out of it relatively early. Two cars of the same age can be treated completely differently.

A salvage brand can also be voluntaryNebraska applies the designation where an owner voluntarily obtains a salvage-branded title, regardless of damage, age or value. So a Nebraska salvage brand does not always mean an insurer made a determination.

Nebraska carries forward other states' brands. A multi-state history shows you the same record.

Brands carry forward, with the source named

All brands must carry forward to later titles and registrations, and out-of-state titles showing damage brands — including Rebuilt — are carried forward with the issuing jurisdiction's name. That naming is useful: it tells you where to look next rather than leaving you with an unattributed word on a document.

It also makes a clean Nebraska title a somewhat stronger signal than a clean title in a state with no carry-forward rule. See title brands and title washing.

Who files, and when

  • An insurer that pays a total loss settlement must have a Salvage Certificate of Title issued in its own name.
  • Where the insured keeps the damaged vehicle, the insurer notifies the DMV electronically, and the insured must obtain a Salvage Certificate of Title within 30 days of settlement.
  • The Salvage Certificate of Title costs $10 and is filed with a County Treasurer alongside the title application.

Getting back on the road

After repair, the vehicle needs an inspection and a Vehicle Inspection Certificate submitted with the title application. The inspection is required before a repaired salvage vehicle can be titled and registered, and before the car is operated on public roads once a Previously Salvaged title has been issued.

Ask for that certificate. As with every state in this series, it establishes identity rather than repair quality — see the pre-purchase inspection for the part nobody else does.

Buying checklist for Nebraska

  1. Look for the Previously Salvaged legend specifically — it is not worded "rebuilt".
  2. Work out whether the car is a late model vehicle by age or by value.
  3. If a brand carried forward, note which jurisdiction is named and check that state's records.
  4. Ask for the Vehicle Inspection Certificate.
  5. Price it as a branded car — see what a salvage title costs you.

Check every jurisdiction named in the title history before you make an offer.
