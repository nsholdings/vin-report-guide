Does the factory warranty transfer to a used-car buyer?

Last checked September 20, 2026

A late-model used car may still be inside its original manufacturer warranty — which can be worth a meaningful amount. Whether that coverage passes to you, and how much of it remains, depends on the manufacturer and on which part of the warranty you are asking about.

Why the answer varies

New-vehicle warranties are set by each manufacturer. They typically include different kinds of coverage with different limits — for example, a general "bumper-to-bumper" warranty and a longer powertrain warranty — and each can have its own time and mileage limits and its own rules about transfer to later owners. Some coverage may be limited to the original owner. There is no single rule that applies to every brand.

How to find out for a specific car

  1. Read the manufacturer's warranty booklet for that model year — the manufacturer usually publishes it on its website. Look for how coverage is described for "subsequent owners".
  2. Find the warranty start date. Coverage usually runs from when the car was first sold or put into service, not from the model year.
  3. Ask a franchised dealer for that brand to look the VIN up. They can typically see the in-service date and any remaining coverage.
  4. Check the mileage against each coverage limit.
Get it in writingIf a seller tells you the car is "still under warranty", confirm it with the manufacturer or a franchised dealer using the VIN. A seller's belief about warranty status is often out of date.

Things that can void or limit coverage

Terms differ by manufacturer, but warranty booklets commonly exclude or limit coverage for some situations — for example, damage from accidents, flood, or misuse, and some manufacturers restrict coverage on vehicles that have received certain title brands. A car with a salvage or rebuilt history may not carry the warranty you expect. Check before you rely on it.

A branded title can affect warranty coverage. Check the title history before you count on the warranty.
