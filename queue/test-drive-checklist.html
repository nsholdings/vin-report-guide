The used-car test drive: what to actually check

Last checked September 20, 2026

Most test drives are ten minutes on a route the seller chose. That tells you the car moves. A test drive done deliberately can tell you much more — and it costs nothing but time.

Before you start the engine

  • Ask for a cold start. Ask the seller not to run the car before you arrive, and put your hand on the hood when you get there. Many problems — hard starting, rough idle, smoke, noises — are most obvious from cold.
  • Check the VIN on the dashboard and door jamb against the title. See VIN basics.
  • Warning lights on startup. With the key on, the dashboard lights should illuminate briefly and then go out. A light that never comes on at all may have been disabled.

At idle

  • Listen for knocking, ticking or whining
  • Look at the exhaust — color and smell
  • Turn the steering lock to lock and listen
  • Run every electrical item: windows, locks, climate control at every setting, lights, wipers, infotainment

On the road

Choose part of the route yourself, and include:

  • A straight, level road — does it track straight with a light grip on the wheel?
  • Hard, safe braking — does it pull to one side, pulse or squeal?
  • Acceleration from low speed — hesitation, slipping, harsh shifts
  • Highway speed — vibration through the wheel or seat, wind noise from poorly fitted panels
  • Rough pavement — clunks and rattles from the suspension
  • A tight parking maneuver — noises at full steering lock

After you stop

  • Look under the car for fresh drips
  • Check the temperature gauge stayed where it should
  • Check for new warning lights
Drive it with the radio offSellers sometimes switch the radio on before you get in. Turn it off. You are there to listen to the car.

What a test drive can't tell you

A car can drive perfectly and still carry a salvage brand, a rolled-back odometer or an unpaid loan. The drive tests the machine. The record tests the history. See the full pre-purchase checklist, and follow up anything suspicious with an independent inspection.

A good test drive doesn't clear the title. Check the record before you talk price.
